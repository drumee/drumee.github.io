<p className="product-tutorial-intro">A visual tour of Drumee: where everything is, and what each part is for.</p>

The screens below show a sample organization, *Northwind Studio*, working on a client project. Your own workspaces will look the same, with your content.

## 1.1 The Main Window

Everything in Drumee is reached from the sidebar on the left. The area on the right shows what you selected.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/home-overview.png" alt="The Drumee main window, with the parts of the sidebar numbered" />
  <figcaption>1.1 The main window</figcaption>
</figure>

| # | Part | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Team / organization switcher | Switch between your personal space and the teams or organizations you belong to. The gear opens their settings. |
| 2 | Inbox | Your conversations: direct chats and workspace chats, with unread counts. |
| 3 | Notifications | Everything that needs your attention: invitations, task assignments, meetings, new messages and files. |
| 4 | Contacts | People you talk to directly, inside or outside your organization. |
| 5 | Marketplace | Apps that extend your workspaces. |
| 6 | My space | **My files** is your personal workspace, visible only to you. **Calendar** gathers your meetings and task deadlines from every workspace. |
| 7 | Workspaces | Your workspaces, grouped into **Internal** and **External**. The **+** creates a new one. |
| 8 | Workspace views | Each workspace has four views: **Files**, **Chat**, **Task** and **Meeting**. |
| 9 | Account menu | Your account settings, language, help, and sign out. |

## 1.2 Account Settings

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Account**.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/account-menu.png" alt="The account menu" />
  <figcaption>1.2 Account menu</figcaption>
</figure>

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/account-settings.png" alt="Account settings: profile, preferences, billing, credentials and danger zone" />
  <figcaption>1.2.1 Account settings</figcaption>
</figure>

1. **General Profile:** your photo, display name, username and a short bio.
2. **Preferences:** display mode (Light, Dark or System), email notifications, two-factor authentication and an authenticator app.
3. **Billing &amp; subscription:** your current plan, and **Manage subscription**.
4. **Account Credentials:** change the email address or the password of your account.
5. **Danger zone:**
   - **Export data** — download your files, chat history and workspace data as a .zip archive.
   - **Delete account** — permanently delete your account and its data.

Further down the same page:

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/account-more.png" alt="Account settings: invite members, import, notifications and sessions" />
  <figcaption>1.2.2 Invite, import, notifications and sessions</figcaption>
</figure>

6. **Invite members:** your referral code and referral link.
7. **Import:** bring your existing files in with **Migrate from Google Drive**.
8. **Notifications:** mute everything, mute single workspaces or people, and turn on desktop notifications.
9. **Sessions:** every device signed in to your account. **Revoke** signs a device out immediately.

## 1.3 Team &amp; Organization Settings

Click the gear next to the switcher to manage the team or organization you are in. What you see depends on your role there and on its plan.

### 1.3.1 Members

Who belongs to the organization, with their role and teams. The panel on the right explains what each role can do.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/org-members.png" alt="The members tab of the organization settings" />
  <figcaption>1.3.1 Members</figcaption>
</figure>

**Invite a member**

Step 1: Open the **Members** tab => Click **Invite people**.

Step 2: Enter one or several email addresses.

Step 3: Choose the **access level** — Member, Viewer, Manager, Security admin or Admin — and, if you like, the **teams** the new members join straight away.

Step 4: Add a personal note => Click **Send invitations**. Each person receives an email and appears under *Pending invitations* until they accept.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/org-invite-members.png" alt="Invite people to the organization" />
  <figcaption>1.3.1 Invite members to the organization</figcaption>
</figure>

To change someone’s role or teams later, use the menus on their row. **Remove** takes them out of the organization.

### 1.3.2 Teams

Teams group people so that you can give them access to workspaces together. A person can be in several teams.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/org-teams.png" alt="The teams tab" />
  <figcaption>1.3.2 Teams</figcaption>
</figure>

**Create a team**

Step 1: Open the **Teams** tab => Click **New team**.

Step 2: Type the team’s name => Press **Enter**.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/org-new-team.png" alt="Name the new team" />
  <figcaption>1.3.2 Create a team</figcaption>
</figure>

Step 3: Click **Members** on the team => Tick the people who belong to it => Click **Save**.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/org-team-members.png" alt="Choose the members of a team" />
  <figcaption>1.3.2 Choose the members of a team</figcaption>
</figure>

The pencil renames a team, the bin deletes it, and the handle on the left changes the order of the teams everywhere they are listed.

### 1.3.3 Workspaces

Every workspace of the organization in one list: who it is visible to, the role people join with, its members, and the workspace trash.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/org-workspaces.png" alt="The workspaces tab" />
  <figcaption>1.3.3 Workspaces</figcaption>
</figure>

**Assign a workspace to teams**

Step 1: Open the **Workspaces** tab => Find the internal workspace.

Step 2: Under **Visible to**, choose who joins it:

- **Everyone in the organization** — every member sees it, now and when they join.
- **Selected teams** — the members of the teams you pick join automatically.
- **Private** — only the people you add by hand.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/org-workspace-visibility.png" alt="Choose who a workspace is visible to" />
  <figcaption>1.3.3 Choose who the workspace is visible to</figcaption>
</figure>

Step 3: With **Selected teams**, click the team menu next to it => Tick the teams.

<figure className="product-tutorial-figure">
  <img src="/img/product-tutorial/org-workspace-teams.png" alt="Assign teams to a workspace" />
  <figcaption>1.3.3 Assign teams to the workspace</figcaption>
</figure>

The members of those teams join the workspace at once, with the role shown under **Joins as**. Removing someone from the team removes them from the workspace too, unless they were added by hand.

## 1.5 Task

The **Task** view of a workspace keeps its work in one place, in six layouts.

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Board | Tasks as cards in columns, from *To do* to *Complete*. |
| List | Every task in a table: priority, status, dates, points, epic, assignee. |
| Backlog | Epics, the current sprint, and what is waiting. |
| Calendar | Tasks on their due date. |
| Timeline | Tasks as bars over time, grouped by epic. |
| Reports | Progress, burndown, velocity and workload. |

### 1.5.1 Board view

Drag a card to another column to change its status. **New task** adds a card; **Labels**, **Filter** and **Activity** are at the top right.
