The world runs on fragmented tools. Files here, chat there, tasks somewhere else - no one owns the data, no one controls the workflow. Broken collaboration isn't a feature problem. It's a structural one.

**Drumee** is the **world's first unified workspace which connects** seamless collaboration, sovereignty, and security in one platform. We don't just store your data - we activate it. Files, chat, and tasks become one living system, under your control, extensible to your needs.

- **More connected:** Turn chats into tasks, discuss files in context, and manage meetings in one workspace.
- **Faster:** Co-edit documents in real time, track project progress, and automate task organization.
- **Lower costs:** Replace separate tools for storage, chat, documents, tasks, and meetings with one platform.
- **More control:** Manage granular permissions, secure sharing, access logs, and self-hosted data ownership.

**5,000+ beta users, 50+ pilots, and 20+ paying customers** - early traction demonstrating real market demand and willingness to pay. Drumee is turning that momentum into a scalable business.

:::note[Where Drumee is today]

Drumee runs today as a hosted service at [app.drumee.com](https://app.drumee.com). The self-hosted edition of the platform is [coming soon](/self-hosting).

:::
